How It Works

Upload your PDF. SigraChain generates a unique SHA-256 cryptographic fingerprint that proves the document hasn't been altered.

Why SigraChain?

Traditional e-signatures depend on a single company. If that company shuts down, your proof disappears. SigraChain provides proof that survives independently.

Public Verification

Anyone — courts, regulators, auditors — can verify document authenticity without needing SigraChain to exist. Upload the file or enter the hash.

Tamper-Evident

If a single byte of the signed document changes, the cryptographic proof fails. Alteration is mathematically detectable and impossible to hide.

Legally Valid

Compliant with ESIGN Act (US), eIDAS (EU), and MP 2.200-2 (Brazil). Three pillars: document integrity, signer intent, complete audit trail.

On-Chain Proof

Each signed batch is anchored as a cryptographic attestation on Base or Arbitrum. Merkle proofs link every document to the on-chain root hash.
